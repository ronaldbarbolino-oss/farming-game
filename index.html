Animals

Buy a male and a female to breed: the female gets pregnant on her own and gives birth after 30 minutes. Each species has its own pen. They shelter on their own when it rains or snows. They can get sick: treat any with a red cross within 5 minutes or it will die.
Resting animals go inside for 3 minutes. After that they can't get sick for 20 minutes.

Animal Houses

Level up a house to keep more animals (+3 space) and give it a makeover: new roof, flowers, a flag, then lanterns and a stone path.
