Animals

Buy a male and a female to breed: the female gets pregnant on her own and gives birth after 30 minutes. Each species has its own pen. They shelter on their own when it rains or snows.
Resting animals go inside for 3 minutes. After that they can't get sick for 20 minutes.

Sell Animals

Sell an animal you don't need. Adults sell for 70% of their price, babies for 35%, sick animals for half of that. Pregnant animals are sold last.

Animal Houses

Level up a house (up to Level 20) to keep more animals and give it a makeover. From Level 5 the yard also gets bigger. Each house has a most it can ever hold. Press 📐 Bigger pen to make the yard wider (+2 space).
